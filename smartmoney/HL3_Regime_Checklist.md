# HL3 Regime Checklist

## Regime check

- price compressed under or over key level?
- fake impulses hit the range edge but do not hold?
- volume builds into expectation, not into acceptance?

Rule:

- if all 3 are present, treat the market as `HL3 regime`

## Long bounce

- support is clear on higher TF
- volatility contracts near support
- rejection candle appears
- do not chase breakout
- main target must still give `RR >= 1.8`

## Short trap

- fake break above range edge
- no real volume acceptance
- price re-enters the range fast
- short only after re-entry / retest
- main target must still give `RR >= 1.8`

## Hard no-trade

- breakout accepted on real volume
- no structural stop
- target exists only to force a nice RR
- regime is unclear
